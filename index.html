Why it matters

Is your business email protecting your business?

Email carries your quotes, invoices and customer conversations. A basic or unmanaged setup leaves all of it exposed.

  • Free or shared mailboxes

    Customers see a generic address, and you cannot manage or recover a mailbox when someone leaves.

  • Phishing and spam

    Without strong filtering, fake invoices and scam links reach your team’s inbox.

  • Passwords alone

    One stolen password can expose every message when multi-factor sign-in is not switched on.

  • No admin control

    Nobody can see who has access or shut down a leaver’s account quickly.
